## Story (verified: Wikipedia "Paint protection film"; industry histories)
PPF began as a 3M project for the US military during the Vietnam War, to protect helicopter rotor blades
from debris (nickname "helicopter tape"); race teams adopted it, then everyday cars; modern films have a
self-healing top coat. Today anyone sells a roll and calls it protection → FILMX installs only the genuine
thing (XPEL, original Ravoony films) and replaces any damaged piece it installed, free.
